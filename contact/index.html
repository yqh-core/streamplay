Contact StreamPlay

Use the StreamPlay issue tracker for playback bugs, product suggestions and rights-related notices. The project is maintained by a small team, so support is asynchronous rather than live chat.

Report a playback problem

Before reporting an issue, review the troubleshooting guide. If the problem persists, include:

  • The stream type (HLS/M3U8 or MP4) and a public test URL, if you can share one safely.
  • Browser name and version, operating system and device type.
  • The exact error shown by StreamPlay.
  • Relevant browser Console messages, with private tokens and personal data removed.

Suggest a feature

Tell us what task you are trying to complete, what currently gets in the way, and how often the feature would help. Suggestions are reviewed by usefulness and implementation effort; a submission does not guarantee a delivery date.

Copyright or legal notice

If you are a rights holder and believe a playback URL is associated with infringing material, use the Copyright & Acceptable Use Policy and submit the URL, a concise explanation of your rights, and a valid contact method.

Contact channel and response time

Open a StreamPlay issue on GitHub. Please do not post private stream URLs, credentials or access tokens in a public issue. Typical response time is 1–7 business days; this is not a guaranteed service level.

For data questions, see the Privacy Policy.
