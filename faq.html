Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about M3U8 playlists, HLS playback and stream troubleshooting

What is an M3U8 URL?

An M3U8 URL points to a text playlist used by HLS. The playlist describes media segments or links to variant playlists; it is not usually a direct video file.

What is the difference between a master playlist and a media playlist?

A master playlist lists available renditions and their attributes. A media playlist lists the segments for one rendition, including their durations and resource paths.

Why does an M3U8 URL work in one player but not in my browser?

The browser may enforce CORS, HTTPS mixed-content, codec or authorization rules that another player does not. Check the failing request in the browser Network panel and inspect the response status and headers.

How do I fix a CORS error?

The stream origin must return appropriate CORS headers for the playlist and every required media resource. A browser player cannot bypass CORS. Configure the server or CDN that serves the stream.

What does a 403 response mean?

The origin understood the request but refused access. The URL may require a token, cookie, authorization header, permitted referrer or an unexpired signature.

What does a 404 response mean?

The requested playlist or segment was not found. The playlist may contain a stale path, a segment may have expired from a live window, or the origin may have moved the resource.

Why are there multiple resolutions in one playlist?

HLS can offer multiple variants for adaptive bitrate playback. The player chooses a rendition based on network conditions, buffer health and device support.

Can StreamPlay play every codec or protected stream?

No. Playback depends on browser, device, codec, encryption and authorization support. The analyzer can help inspect playlist structure, but it cannot make an unsupported codec decodable or bypass DRM and access controls.

Why does a live playlist contain only a few segments?

Many live streams use a rolling playlist window that lists only recent segments. Older segments may be removed as the live stream advances.

Does StreamPlay host or proxy my video?

No. StreamPlay is a browser-based playlist analyzer and player interface. It does not provide video hosting or a streaming CDN. The stream origin remains responsible for availability, authorization and CORS.
