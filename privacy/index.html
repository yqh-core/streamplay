Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In brief: StreamPlay plays media in your browser and does not operate a media proxy or store uploaded video files. The website is hosted by Cloudflare and uses Google AdSense. Optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) loads only after you grant analytics consent.

1. Stream URLs and playback requests

When you paste a stream URL into the player, your browser requests the playlist and media segments directly from the host named in that URL. Those media requests do not pass through a StreamPlay media server, and StreamPlay does not store or record the media content.

Important exception: if you open the player using the optional ?url= parameter, the supplied address is included in the page request URL. The hosting provider may process that request URL in its access logs. Avoid putting passwords, private tokens or other secrets in stream URLs or public iframe links.

2. Information processed by the website

  • Hosting and security logs: Cloudflare may process your IP address, browser and device information, request time, requested path and other standard request metadata to deliver and protect the site.
  • Theme preference: the site may store your light/dark theme preference in browser local storage.
  • Consent choice: your analytics consent preference is stored so the site can respect your choice on later visits.
  • Support messages: if you contact us through GitHub, the information you submit is processed by GitHub under its own policies.

StreamPlay does not require account registration and does not maintain a reader account database.

3. Google AdSense

Google AdSense is enabled on the site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process IP address, browser or device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions to deliver, measure, limit or personalize advertising, subject to your location, applicable consent requirements and Google's settings. You can review or change ad personalization through Google Ads Settings and read the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Optional Google Analytics 4

The site offers optional GA4 page-view analytics. GA4 is loaded only after you select “Accept Google Analytics”. If you reject analytics, the site should not send GA4 requests. The configured page-view data is limited to page path, page title and referrer domain; it is not intended to include the stream URL, media content or URL query parameters. You can change your choice at any time through “Privacy choices”.

5. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, CDN delivery and security processing.
  • Google — AdSense advertising and optional GA4 analytics.
  • GitHub — project hosting and issue-based support, if you choose to contact us there.

When you play a stream, the stream provider also receives requests directly from your browser and may process your IP address and request metadata under its own privacy policy. StreamPlay does not control that provider.

6. Retention

  • Stream media and playlist contents are not stored by a StreamPlay media backend.
  • Theme and consent preferences remain in your browser until cleared or replaced.
  • Cloudflare retains hosting and security logs according to its service policies and configuration.
  • Google retains advertising and analytics data according to its policies, settings and applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can reject or change optional GA4 consent through “Privacy choices”, clear browser storage and cookies, manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings, or stop using the site. For questions about information held by Cloudflare or Google, use the relevant provider's privacy request process. For questions about StreamPlay itself, use the Contact page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its third-party services change. The revision date at the top of this page indicates the latest update.
